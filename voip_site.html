Pricing Questions

What counts as a "minute"?
Calls are billed per minute with 6-second rounding after the first 30 seconds. A 90-second call is billed as 1.5 minutes. The rate is per minute for the destination country.
Can I top up calling credits separately?
Yes. You can purchase additional calling credits anytime from your dashboard in multiples of ₹500. Credits never expire while your plan is active.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — we offer ₹50 free calling credits on signup, no credit card required. You can test call quality to any destination before committing.
Can I switch plans later?
Absolutely. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply from the next billing cycle. Any unused monthly credits carry forward during an upgrade.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer? Contact our support team.

How do I get started with VoIP Infotech? +
Sign up for a free account, choose your plan, and you'll receive ₹50 in free calling credits. No credit card required to start. Download the web dialler or configure your SIP client and you're ready to call in under 5 minutes.
Do I need any special hardware? +
No special hardware needed. VoIP Infotech works with our web browser dialler, our mobile app (iOS & Android), or any SIP-compatible phone or softphone. If you're upgrading from a traditional PBX, we provide free migration support.
How long does activation take? +
Account activation is instant. SIP credentials are delivered within 60 seconds of signup. For enterprise accounts with custom number provisioning, it may take up to 24 hours.
How am I billed for calls? +
Calls are billed per minute with 6-second rounding after the first 30 seconds. Credits are deducted in real time from your wallet. Monthly plan fees are billed on your renewal date. We provide itemized CDR reports downloadable as CSV.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI, Net Banking, all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and NEFT/RTGS for enterprise accounts. GST invoices are issued automatically for all transactions.
Do unused credits roll over? +
Yes. Calling credits never expire while your plan is active. If you cancel your plan, any remaining credits can be used for 30 days after cancellation.
What codecs and protocols do you support? +
We support G.711 (a-law / μ-law), G.729, and Opus codecs. Signaling uses SIP over TLS; media is encrypted with SRTP. We're compatible with Asterisk, FreePBX, 3CX, Zoiper, and all standard SIP clients.
What internet speed do I need? +
Each simultaneous call requires approximately 100 kbps upload and download (G.711). A 10 Mbps connection can comfortably handle 50+ concurrent calls. We recommend a stable, low-latency connection (under 150ms round-trip to our nearest PoP).
Do you have an API? +
Yes. Our REST API (available on Business and Enterprise plans) lets you initiate calls, retrieve CDRs, manage numbers, and receive real-time call events via webhooks. Full documentation is available after signup.
Which countries can I call? +
VoIP Infotech connects to landlines and mobiles in 200+ countries. Check our live Rates page for per-minute pricing by country. Rates update in real time as carrier costs change.
Can I keep my existing phone number? +
Yes, number porting is available for landline numbers in India and select international numbers. The process typically takes 7–14 business days. Contact our support team to initiate a port request.
Will the person I'm calling see my Indian number? +
By default, your registered VoIP Infotech DID is displayed. Business and Enterprise plans include custom caller ID — you can display a local number for the destination country to improve answer rates.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including name, email address, phone number, company name, and billing information when you create an account or contact us. We also collect usage data such as call detail records (CDRs), login timestamps, and IP addresses.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the VoIP Infotech service, process payments, send transactional emails (account confirmations, invoices, support replies), and comply with legal obligations under Indian law including TRAI regulations.

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We share data only with payment processors and cloud infrastructure providers under strict data processing agreements.
  • Call records are retained for 90 days unless a longer period is required by law.

3. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Voice media is encrypted with SRTP. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. We are ISO 27001 certified and undergo annual third-party security audits.

4. Your Rights

Under the IT Act 2000 and applicable rules, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email privacy@voipinfotech.com. We respond within 30 days.

5. Cookies

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies for session management and analytics cookies (with your consent) to understand how visitors use our site. You may opt out of analytics cookies via your browser settings.

6. Contact

For privacy-related queries, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@voipinfotech.com or write to: VoIP Infotech, Plot 42, Sector 63, Noida, UP 201301, India.
