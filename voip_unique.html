What is VoIP and how does it work for international calls?

VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) converts your voice into digital data packets and transmits them over the internet to any phone number worldwide. Because it uses existing internet infrastructure instead of traditional phone lines, international VoIP calling from India costs a fraction of regular telephone rates — often 70–90% cheaper than BSNL or Airtel international calls.

How cheap are international calls with VoIP Infotech?

VoIP Infotech offers some of India's lowest international calling rates: USA and Canada from ₹0.60/min, UK from ₹0.75/min, UAE from ₹1.80/min, Singapore from ₹1.20/min, and competitive rates to 200+ countries. All rates are published live on our website with no hidden charges or connection fees.

Do I need any special hardware to use VoIP Infotech?

No special hardware is required to get started. You can use our free web dialler from any browser, or our mobile app on Android and iOS. If you have an existing IP phone, FreePBX, Asterisk, or 3CX system, simply add our SIP credentials as a trunk — setup takes under 5 minutes with our step-by-step guides.

What is a SIP trunk and does VoIP Infotech provide SIP trunking in India?

A SIP trunk connects your existing PBX or IP phone system to the public telephone network over the internet, replacing traditional ISDN or PRI lines. VoIP Infotech offers SIP trunking from ₹499/month with unlimited scalable channels — ideal for call centres, BPOs, and businesses with high international call volumes. We support SIP 2.0 and are compatible with all major IP-PBX platforms.

Is VoIP Infotech compliant with Indian telecom regulations (TRAI)?

Yes. VoIP Infotech operates in full compliance with TRAI (Telecom Regulatory Authority of India) guidelines and is a registered telecom service provider. We have been operating legally since 2010. All calls are encrypted with TLS and SRTP to protect your business communications from interception.

What happens if my internet connection goes down during a call?

VoIP Infotech supports call failover — your calls can be automatically redirected to a traditional mobile number if your internet connection drops. Our uptime SLA is 99.9%, backed by redundant infrastructure across multiple India data centres and automatic route failover to backup carriers.
